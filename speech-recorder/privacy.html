Speech Recorder: privacy policy

Speech Recorder records audio on your phone and, if you choose, uploads it to your own Google Drive. It has no server of its own: nothing you record, and nothing about you, is sent to the app's developers or to anyone else.

What the app records and keeps

  • Audio, only whilst you are recording, from your headset's microphone or the phone's own. It is saved on the phone, in one-minute files, in a folder per session.
  • A list of each session's files, with their lengths, sizes and checksums, kept in the app's private storage.

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Google Drive

If you tap Connect Google Drive and allow access, the app uploads your recordings to a Speech Recorder folder in your own Drive. It asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it created itself, never anything else in your Drive. A recording is removed from the phone once Drive confirms it holds an exact copy; the app never deletes anything from Drive.

Sign-in is handled by Google Play services on your phone. You can withdraw the app's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections; recordings then stay on the phone. Google's handling of your Drive is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Speech Recorder's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • Microphone, to record.
  • Nearby devices, to use a Bluetooth headset's microphone.
  • Notifications, to show that recording is running and to raise alarms.
  • Network, only to upload to your Google Drive.
  • Alarms, so a watchdog can warn you if recording stops unexpectedly.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it keeps on the phone. Recordings already uploaded stay in your Drive until you delete them there.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the releases repository.
